# Example: a simple request

Shows the skill *not* adding process. Most requests land here.

## Request

> What's the difference between gross margin and contribution margin?

## Tier

Simple. Definition question, no branching, no deliverable.

## What the skill does

- No plan, no clarifying question, no restatement of the question.
- Answers directly, with one concrete example because the distinction is easiest to see numerically.
- One silent accuracy check: the definitions are right and both were covered.
- Stops.

## Good response shape

> Gross margin subtracts only cost of goods sold. Contribution margin subtracts all variable costs, including variable costs that sit outside COGS - sales commission, payment processing, shipping.
>
> On £100 of revenue with £40 COGS and £10 of commission and fees: gross margin is £60 (60%), contribution margin is £50 (50%).
>
> Gross margin is the reporting figure. Contribution margin is the decision figure - it tells you what each additional sale actually contributes to fixed costs.

## Failure modes this avoids

| Anti-pattern | What it would have looked like |
|---|---|
| Ceremony on a small task | "Let me plan how to approach this comparison..." |
| Restating the question | "You're asking about the difference between two margin metrics." |
| Clarifying question with no branch | "Are you asking in a SaaS or retail context?" - the definitions are the same either way |
| Option dumping | Listing five margin metrics when two were asked about |

## Variant: simple but with a deliverable

> Write me a two-line out-of-office for next week.

Still simple. Write the two lines in the chat. No artifact - it is two lines. One check: does it say the dates and who to contact? If the user did not supply either, write it with a bracketed placeholder rather than asking.
